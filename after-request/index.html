    What happens next

    A real person reads every request

    Sending a request does not start a hold. Our team checks your dates first. If the stay fits, we place a hold and send payment details. Your booking is confirmed once we've matched your payment.

    Illustrative photo — our own photography follows

    01

    1 · We check and hold

    We check the room or whole-house dates you asked for. If the stay fits, we hold it for 30 minutes for Alipay or WeChat Pay, or 24 hours for Taiwan bank transfer.

    02

    2 · You hear from us

    We reply with your booking reference in the form YL-NNNN. If your first choice is no longer available, we suggest alternatives.

    03

    3 · Payment

    We send payment details with your YL- reference. You pay the full amount when you book, by Taiwan bank transfer, Alipay or WeChat Pay. The booking is confirmed once we've matched the payment. Please never send card or passport details in chat.
