    House rules

    RYO HOUSE

    House rules

    From our stay guide v1.7 (November 2026).

    Check-in and check-out

    • Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 10:00. Leave your room key in the room.
    • No late check-out. Skiing on your last day? Store your things in the basement and collect them the same day. The lodge is off-limits after check-out.
    • Self check-in: the front door has an electronic lock, and we send the code before you arrive. Your room key is inside. Tell us if you arrive after 21:00, and message us once you are in.
    • Please show your passport or ID at check-in. No unregistered overnight guests.

    Rooms and guests

    • 4 tatami rooms: 2 guests as standard, 3 at most. 2 Western rooms: 4 as standard, 5 at most.
    • Each guest above the standard number is ¥8,000 per night, declared before check-in.

    Gear

    • Skis go in the outdoor rack. No shoes or ski boots indoors. The drying room at the entrance dries your gear overnight.

    Kitchen, bath and heating

    • The kitchen is free to use. Clean up after yourselves and save water and power. No breakfast is served.
    • The bathroom is shared: clear your things after use. We provide bath towels, body wash and shampoo. Bring your own toothbrush and toothpaste. Message our official LINE to change towels.
    • If a heater runs out of kerosene, message our official LINE and we will refill it.

    House manners

    • Sort trash by the labels on the bins. Quiet after 22:00.
    • No smoking anywhere indoors; a ¥30,000 cleaning and deodorising fee applies. No pets.

    Damage and costs

    • Please look after the furniture and equipment and report problems at once. Damage from carelessness to equipment, furniture, linen, appliances or the building is charged at cost; linen cleaning is ¥2,000. A lost room key is ¥10,000.
    • No compensation if force majeure (natural disaster, heavy snow) puts equipment out of use.

    Payment and cancellation

    • Pay by Taiwan bank transfer, Alipay, WeChat Pay or Singapore PayNow.
    • Refund: 80% 30 or more days before check-in, 40% 21–29 days, 10% 14–20 days, none within 14 days or for a no-show. Full refund for force majeure confirmed by both sides.

    Winter travel

    • Check JR East announcements before you travel. Tell us if you will arrive after 21:00.

    Winter safety

    • Stay inside the ski-area boundaries during avalanche or blizzard warnings. Go into the backcountry only with a certified local guide and a full avalanche kit.
    • In an emergency, call 119 first. If someone is hurt, keep them warm and still, then contact us.
