    Before you book

    The terms that matter, in plain language. Each summary follows the full booking terms you accept.

    • Payment: the full amount when you book. Cancellation refunds of the amount paid: 80% 30 or more days before check-in, 40% 21–29 days before, 10% 14–20 days before, and no refund under 14 days or for a no-show. Force majeure confirmed by both sides is refunded in full.
    • Taiwan bank transfer, Alipay, WeChat Pay or Singapore PayNow. Prices are quoted in JPY. Confirm your dates and payment method in chat and we hold your room right away, 30 minutes for Alipay, WeChat Pay or PayNow, 24 hours for Taiwan bank transfer. Payment details and the QR code arrive in the chat. Your booking is confirmed once we've matched your payment.
    • We refund the amount paid according to when you cancel: 30 days or more before check-in, 80%; 21–29 days, 40%; 14–20 days, 10%; under 14 days or a no-show, no refund. If both sides confirm force majeure, we refund in full. We count calendar days in Japan time from the cancellation date to the check-in date. Terms version 2026-11-10.
    • Check-in from 15:00. Check-out by 10:00, and late check-out is not available. Skiing on your last day? Leave all your belongings in the basement storage and collect them the same day.
