    House rules

    Arrival

    8–10 min walk from Echigo-Yuzawa Station; ~3 min by taxi. Parking in front of the house

    Check-in & door

    Check-in 15:00, check-out 10:00. Code sent before arrival; key inside. Please present your passport at check-in. No unregistered overnight guests

    Wi-Fi

    Name: Ryohouse

    Password: ••••••••

    Gear & boots

    Skis in the outdoor rack; no shoes or boots indoors; drying room at the entrance

    Kitchen & heating

    Kitchen open to guests — clean up, save water and power. If a heater runs out of kerosene, message our official LINE and we'll refill it

    Bath & amenities

    Shared bathroom — clear your things after use. Provided: bath towels, body wash, shampoo. Toothbrush & toothpaste: bring your own; towel change via LINE

    Trash & quiet

    Sort trash by bin labels. Quiet after 22:00 — please don't disturb other guests

    No smoking

    Smoking is prohibited indoors. Violation: JPY 30,000 cleaning fee

    Gear, coaching & shuttle

    Gear rental and coaching on request. Paid shuttle to the ski resorts via our partner — please ask in advance

    Check-out & storage

    Check out by 10:00; leave the key in the room. No late check-out. Guests skiing that day may store belongings in the basement. Lodge facilities are off-limits after check-out

    Rooms & extra guests

    4 tatami rooms (standard 2, max 3); 2 Western rooms (standard 4, max 5). Extra guest JPY 8,000/night, declared before check-in. For room rates, please check our official website or ask via our official LINE account

    Damages & compensation

    Handle the furniture and equipment with care; report any issues to our official account. Damage from negligence is charged at cost; linen cleaning JPY 2,000. Lost room key: JPY 10,000. No compensation for force-majeure outages

    Notes

    No pets. Quiet after 22:00

    Snow & delays

    Check JR East announcements before departing. Tell us if you'll arrive after 21:00

    Late arrival

    Enter with your pre-sent door code; the key is in the room. Just message us when you're in
