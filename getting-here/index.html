    01

    From Echigo-Yuzawa Station

    It is about 700 m: roughly a 10-minute walk, longer with luggage or in snow, or 2–3 minutes by car.

    • About 700 m from Echigo-Yuzawa Station — roughly a 10-minute walk, longer with luggage or in snow; 2–3 minutes by car. Taxis are usually available at the station; it is a short ride.
    • Taxis are usually available at Echigo-Yuzawa Station; it is a short ride to WhitePowder.
    • Station pickup add-on: a car meets you at Echigo-Yuzawa Station, West Exit. Tell us your train's arrival time the day before. After 20:00 there is a ¥5,000 night charge. Ask us for the pickup price.

    02

    Ski shuttle

    The Kagura car follows a fixed timetable from WhitePowder. Ask our concierge about other ski areas.

    • Our ski-shuttle car collects you at WhitePowder for Kagura at 07:50, 08:50, 15:30 and 16:30. For other ski areas, ask our concierge.
