    RYO HOUSE · YUZAWA

    Location

    1892-34 Yuzawa, Minamiuonuma District, Niigata 949-6101, Japan

    Open in Google MapsOpen in Apple Maps

    01

    From Echigo-Yuzawa Station

    It's about 700 m: an 8–10 minute walk, a little longer with luggage or in snow, or about 3 minutes by taxi. Carrying a lot? Take a taxi from the station, book our pickup or ask us for a charter ride.

    • Taxis usually wait at Echigo-Yuzawa Station, and the ride to RYO House takes about 3 minutes. We can also arrange a charter ride on request.
    • Station pickup add-on: a car meets you at Echigo-Yuzawa Station, West Exit. Tell us your train's arrival time the day before. After 20:00 there is a ¥5,000 night charge. Ask us for the pickup price.

    02

    Straight to Kagura

    Our shuttle car leaves RYO House for Kagura on a fixed timetable, so nobody has to drive to the slopes. Heading to another ski area? Ask our concierge.

    • Our ski-shuttle car collects you at RYO House for Kagura at 07:50, 08:50, 15:30 and 16:30. For other ski areas, ask our concierge.

    03

    Arrival day

    Check in from 15:00 with the door code we send before you arrive; your room key is waiting inside your room. Check-out is by 10:00. Look for the 稜 RYO sign at the entrance.

    • Self check-in, any time from 15:00. The front door has an electronic lock; we send you the code privately before you arrive, and your room key is waiting inside your room. Arriving late? Let yourself in and just message us. Please tell us if you will arrive after 21:00, and in heavy snow check JR East service notices before you travel.
    • On check-out day you can leave all your belongings in the basement storage while you ski, then collect them the same day.

    04

    Driving or arriving late?

    Arriving late is fine: let yourself in and message us. If you'll arrive after 21:00, tell us in advance, and in heavy snow check JR East service notices before you set off. Driving? Message our concierge and we'll go over the last stretch with you.
