    Five rooms in Ishijiro, Yuzawa

    Five rooms under one roof

    Step off the Shinkansen at Echigo-Yuzawa and walk about ten minutes to our door. Ski all day, then gather your group around the low tables in the tatami living room.

    Two large rooms sleep four and three smaller rooms sleep two. Any room takes one extra bed. Book a single room, or take the whole house for 14 guests, up to 19 with extra beds.

    • 249.06 m² registered floor area
    • 3 registered storeys
    • Registered use: dwelling
    • Registered new-build date: 1 August 1975
    • 5 guest rooms
    • All 5 rooms share 2 toilets and 2 showers.
