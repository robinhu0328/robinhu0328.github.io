    Five rooms under one roof

    Step off the Shinkansen at Echigo-Yuzawa, walk 8–10 minutes to our door and let yourself in with the code we send before you arrive. Ski all day, leave your gear in the drying room overnight, and gather your group around the low tables in the tatami living room.

    Two large rooms sleep four and three smaller rooms sleep two. Any room takes one extra bed. Book a single room, or take the whole house for 14 guests, up to 19 with extra beds.

    • Your backcountry powder base in Echigo-Yuzawa, an 8–10 minute walk from the station
    • 249.06 m² registered floor area

    • Registered use: dwelling
    • Registered new-build date: 1 August 1975
    • 5 guest rooms
    • There is no onsen. All 5 rooms share 2 toilets and 2 showers.

    FAQ

    What guests ask before booking

    When are check-in and check-out?

    Check-in from 15:00. Check-out by 10:00, and late check-out is not available. Skiing on your last day? Leave all your belongings in the basement storage and collect them the same day.

    How do we reach the house?

    About 700 m from Echigo-Yuzawa Station: an 8–10 minute walk, a little longer with luggage or in snow, or about 3 minutes by taxi.

    What is the cancellation policy?

    We refund the amount paid according to when you cancel: 30 days or more before check-in, 80%; 21–29 days, 40%; 14–20 days, 10%; under 14 days or a no-show, no refund. If both sides confirm force majeure, we refund in full. We count calendar days in Japan time from the cancellation date to the check-in date. Terms version 2026-11-10.
